About

I'm a Business Application Analyst at a software company in Edmonton, where I look after 70-odd SaaS tools — the integrations, access, renewals, and the unglamorous work of keeping them all talking to each other.

Nights and weekends I build apps for problems I actually have, which is why nearly all of them are about disc golf. Away from a screen: league nights and field work, a couple of planted tanks with shrimp and corydoras, more pepper plants than the garden has room for, and two shelties.
